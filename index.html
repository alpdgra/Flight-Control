How to play

The job
  1. Draw a route. Press an aircraft and drag a line to its landing zone. Release, and it flies the line you drew.
  2. Match the colours. Each zone only takes the aircraft shown on its badge. A route to the wrong zone simply will not stick.
  3. Runways run one way. Drag onto a strip and the aircraft enters at the near end and runs its whole length. Coming in side-on it curves round the threshold to line up — a tight turn onto the runway, never a trip out and back.
  4. Nothing leaves. An aircraft with no route flies straight on and turns back at the boundary. Draw loops to park it while you deal with traffic elsewhere.
  5. Keep them apart. A red ring means two aircraft are closing. If they touch, the shift is over.
Who lands where

Straight lines are quick, sharp corners are quicker than curves, and the fast jets are always the ones to move out of the way.
